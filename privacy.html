Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This policy explains what data this website and Sunny Lane Uploader ("the Tool") handle. The short version: the Tool touches only our own accounts, stores what it needs on one local machine, and collects nothing from visitors or viewers.

1. This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising scripts, and has no forms. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested page) for security and operational purposes, under its own policy.

2. What the Tool accesses

When the operator authorizes the Tool through a platform's OAuth flow, it can access, for the account that granted access:

  • Basic profile information — the account's identifier, display name, and avatar, used to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Profile and account statistics — follower, like, and video counts, used to track how the channel is doing.
  • The account's own video list and post metadata — titles, descriptions, publication dates, and view/like/comment counts for posts on that account.
  • Publishing permission — the ability to upload a video file to that account, either as a draft or as a post.

It requests nothing about any other user, and it does not read other people's profiles, followers, private messages, or feeds.

3. What is stored, and where

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored in a file on the operator's own computer, readable only by that user account. They are never transmitted anywhere except back to the issuing platform.
  • Post metadata and statistics retrieved for our own videos are written to local files (JSON/CSV) on that same computer, so we can review performance over time.
  • Video files we publish are our own, stored locally and uploaded to the platform.

There is no server, no database, and no cloud storage operated by us. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising or profiling.

4. Third parties

The Tool communicates only with the APIs of the platforms we publish to — currently TikTok, YouTube (Google), and Instagram (Meta). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies: TikTok, Google, and Meta.

5. Retention and deletion

Local exports and tokens are kept only as long as they are useful and can be deleted at any time by removing the files. Authorization can be revoked from the platform's own settings, which immediately ends the Tool's access.

6. Children

Our cartoons are made for young children, but this site and the Tool are not directed at children and collect no information from them. Viewers watch our videos inside YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, where those platforms' own children's-privacy protections apply. We label our uploads as children's content where the platform provides that setting.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about visitors or viewers, there is normally nothing to access, correct, or erase. If you believe we hold data about you, write to us and we will respond.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the revision date at the top always reflects the current version.
